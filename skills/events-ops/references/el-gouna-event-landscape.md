# El Gouna: the event landscape

The annual rhythm, the event classes, and the operational realities of running
events in this town. Load this when planning, advising on timing, or sanity
checking whether a plan is realistic.

Researched September 2026 from elgouna.com, the festival's own listings,
Fly Events (the watersports organiser), and press coverage of GFF.

---

## What kind of town this is

El Gouna is a Red Sea resort town developed by Orascom, roughly 25 to 30 minutes
north of Hurghada. It is not a city with an events venue: it is a town where
**the whole place is the venue**. Events happen in the marina, on the water, in
hotel grounds, on the golf course, in the squares and on the beaches.

Consequences that shape every plan:

- **You cannot run two things on the same stretch of water or the same marina
  berth at once.** Neither can you run two things on the same piece of beach in
  the same week, because the build and the get-out take days.
- **Everything arrives from outside.** Production, AV, staging and most talent
  come from Cairo or Hurghada. Talent and guests fly into **Hurghada
  International (HRG)**. Lead times are long and the drive from Cairo is about
  five hours.
- **The wind is a genuine operational factor**, not small talk. The town's
  biggest recurring event class is wind-driven (kitesurfing, wingfoil, sailing).
- **Peak season is October to April.** Christmas, New Year and Easter are the
  peaks. Summer is hot, quieter, and dominated by a domestic and Red Sea diving
  crowd.

## The three event classes

These need different treatment. Treating them as one category is the most common
planning mistake here.

### 1. Town signature events

Large, multi-venue, multi-day, international, approval-heavy.

| Event | Typical timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **El Gouna Film Festival (GFF)** | October, 9 days (2026: 15 to 23 Oct, 9th edition) | The town's flagship. Multiple venues, international talent and press, runs on Eventival (its own festival management system). Founded 2017 by Naguir Sawiris with Bushra Rozza and Amr Mansi. |
| **El Gouna Boat Show** | October | At the New Marina. Marine industry, luxury. |
| **The El Gouna Federation Cup** | Early November (2026: 4 Nov) | Sporting. |
| **El Gouna Half Marathon** | Late November (2026: 21 Nov) | Road closures, medical, water stations. |

Planning posture: months of lead time, multiple authorities, formal permits,
international logistics, contingency planning.

### 2. Watersports and action events

Wind-dependent. Cash prizes. Athlete-facing.

| Event | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **El Gouna Watersports Festival (GWSF)** | September, ~10 days (2026: 16 to 26 Sep, Vol. 6) | The umbrella. Organised by Fly Events. |
| **Kitemania** | within GWSF | Kitesurfing competition, all riding levels. |
| **Windmania** | within GWSF | Windsurfing. |
| **Wakemania** | within GWSF | At **Sliders Cable Park** (2026: 25 Sep). |
| **Kayak & SUPball** | within GWSF | 2026: 26 Sep. |
| **El Gouna Kitemania** | annual | Standalone annual kitesurfing event. |

2026 prize pool: **300,000 EGP** across the disciplines. Athletes register in
advance, so the rider list is a live dependency, not an afterthought.

Planning posture: **check the wind forecast before anything else.** A watersports
event with no wind is a cancelled event. Cash prizes mean sponsor and finance
handling. Athlete registration, safety cover on the water, and rescue boats are
hard requirements.

### 3. Hotel and recurring programming

High frequency, low permit load, the lifeblood of the town's calendar.

Live examples from the town's own listings:

- Cook's Club: daily pool parties, boat parties, Egyptian BBQ nights
- Casa Cook: Bedouin parties, Friday BBQ nights, seafood buffets
- Sheraton Miramar: seafood paradise buffet
- Steigenberger Golf Resort: The Golden Hour
- Three Corners Ocean View: Sunday breakfast
- Ali Pasha Hotel: live singers at Tandoor
- Ancient Sands: Oriental Night
- The Chedi: evening BBQ
- Sultan Bey: BBQ with jazz nights
- **The Marina Street Festival** — weekly, throughout summer, live music and
  shows, held right in front of Captains Inn at the marina. Recurring, not a
  one-off, and easy to forget when planning because it never appears as a
  "new" event.

Planning posture: these are **recurring**, so they should be modelled as a
series rather than re-entered each week. They still consume venue capacity and
still clash.

## Seasonality

| Period | Character | Planning implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Oct to Apr** | Peak. GFF, Boat Show, Federation Cup, Half Marathon. | Multiple events at once, resource contention. The real risk period. |
| **Dec to Jan** | Highest occupancy. Christmas and New Year programming. | Hotel programming peaks, town is full, everything competes for the same suppliers. |
| **Easter** | Second peak. | Same contention, shorter window. |
| **May to Sep** | Hot, quieter. Watersports season (GWSF in September). Domestic and diving crowd. | Wind matters most now. Marina Street Festival runs through the summer. |

## Operational realities

- **Approvals.** Town-wide events touch Orascom's own approvals plus the venue
  plus the relevant authorities. Nothing town-wide happens without several
  sign-offs, and they are the most common source of delay.
- **Residents.** Noise, access and parking complaints are real and have to be
  designed for, not dealt with afterwards.
- **Security.** Large gatherings need a security plan and, depending on size, the
  authorities. This is not optional in Egypt for a public event of scale.
- **Water safety.** Any water-based event needs rescue cover, and that has to be
  booked like any other supplier.
- **Wind and weather.** Open-air evening events can be hit even in calm season.
  Watersports events live or die on the forecast.
- **Payments and currency.** Local suppliers often work on account through the
  season. Cairo production houses quote per project and want deposits. Currency
  is EGP; foreign talent and marine suppliers may quote in USD or EUR.

## How to use this

- When Hisham mentions an event, **place it in one of the three classes** first.
  That determines the checklist.
- When advising on a date, check it against this calendar. Two signature events
  close together is a resourcing problem, not just a busy week.
- When a watersports event is raised, **raise wind and water safety unprompted**.
- When a recurring item appears, ask whether it is already in the system as a
  series rather than creating another one-off.
- Never state a date, a prize, a fee or an attendance figure from this document
  as if it were current. It is a researched snapshot. Confirm the live figure
  with Hisham or the organiser before it goes into a plan.
